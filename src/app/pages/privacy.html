Log tecnici del server

Come ogni sito web, il server che pubblica le pagine registra per ogni richiesta l'indirizzo IP, la data e l'ora, la pagina o il file richiesto, il browser e il sistema (user agent) e la pagina di provenienza (referrer). Questi dati servono per la sicurezza e per risolvere problemi tecnici. Non sono usati per profilazione né per statistiche continuative, non sono incrociati con altri dati né ceduti a terzi, e sono cancellati automaticamente dopo 30 giorni al massimo. Possono essere consultati occasionalmente in forma aggregata (per esempio quante pagine sono state aperte in un giorno), senza conservare gli indirizzi IP, per capire se il sito funziona e viene usato.

Base giuridica: legittimo interesse alla sicurezza e al buon funzionamento del servizio (art. 6, par. 1, lett. f del GDPR). Il server è ospitato da Hetzner Online GmbH (Germania) in un data center in Finlandia, quindi nell'Unione europea; il fornitore mette a disposizione l'infrastruttura e non usa questi dati per altri scopi. I file che usi negli strumenti non arrivano mai al server.

Data controller

Roberto Russo (rrstudio). For any question or request about your data write to {{ email }}.

In short

  • The tools run entirely in your browser: the files and texts you enter are never sent to or stored on the server.
  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Nothing is loaded from third-party servers: fonts and scripts are served by this same site.
  • The Content Security Policy prevents the page from contacting other servers. Anyone can check this with the browser's developer tools (Network tab).

What stays in your browser

A few preferences are saved in localStorage, on your device only, and are never sent to anyone:

  • rrtools.theme: the light or dark theme you chose;
  • rrtools.hint.en: whether you closed the "available in English" bar;
  • rrstudio.markdown-editor.draft: the Markdown editor draft, only if you use it.

To remove them use the "Clear draft" button (for the draft) or clear the site data from your browser settings.

Technical server logs

Like any website, the server that delivers the pages records, for each request, the IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, the browser and system (user agent) and the referring page (referrer). These data are used for security and to fix technical problems. They are not used for profiling or for ongoing statistics, they are not combined with other data or passed to third parties, and they are deleted automatically after 30 days at most. They may occasionally be looked at in aggregate form (for example how many pages were opened in a day), without keeping IP addresses, to understand whether the site works and is being used.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in the security and proper functioning of the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The server is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) in a data center in Finland, within the European Union; the provider only supplies the infrastructure and does not use these data for other purposes. The files you use in the tools never reach the server.

External links

Your rights
